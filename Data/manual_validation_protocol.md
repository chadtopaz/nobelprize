# Verification Protocol — Manual-Validation Sample (n = 200)

**For:** student verifiers · **Owner:** Chad Topaz · **Feeds:** supplement §S7 + response letter (R2-5, R3-5)

## What this is

A stratified random sample of 200 archive people who could NOT be auto-matched to
Wikidata. An AI-assisted search pass (Claude, 2026-08-06) has proposed a resolution
for each name in `proposed_resolutions_200.csv`. **Nothing enters the paper until a
human verifies it.** Your job is the verification, ~1 minute per HIGH row, a few
minutes for MED/UNRESOLVED. Total ≈ 3–4 hours split across the team.

The supplement will describe this protocol exactly as performed: candidate
identifications generated by systematic AI-assisted search of Wikidata, Wikipedia,
and biographical sources, each independently verified or rejected by a member of
the research team. Do not skip rows; do not confirm without checking.

## Per-row procedure

Open the row's archive page: `https://www.nobelprize.org/nomination/archive/show_people.php?id=<person_id>`

- **HIGH / MED rows:** open the cited source in `evidence_notes` (Wikipedia/Wikidata/
  other). Check that the proposed identity matches the archive person on all
  available context: era, country, field/role, and (if the archive page shows one)
  who they nominated or were nominated by. Then set
  `verdict_CONFIRM_REJECT_AMEND`:
  - `CONFIRM` — identity correct as proposed;
  - `AMEND` — right person, but fix a field (birth year/country); put corrections in `verifier_notes`;
  - `REJECT` — wrong person or not defensible; say why in `verifier_notes`.
- **UNRESOLVED rows:** give it one honest attempt (a few minutes, any sources you
  like — library databases welcome; several notes contain a "possibly X" lead worth
  checking first). If you resolve it, fill the resolved_* fields, set verdict
  `AMEND`, and note your source. Otherwise set `CONFIRM` (i.e., you confirm it is
  not readily resolvable).
- **ORG rows:** confirm the entry is indeed an organization and the identification
  is right.
- Rows whose notes flag an archive-country conflict (e.g., Mazurkiewicz
  Germany/Poland; de los Ríos Mexico; Negulesco El Salvador): note what you find —
  these are informative about archive data quality.
- Always fill `verifier_initials`.

## When done

Return the completed CSV to Chad → Claude computes the final §S7 statistics
(verified resolution rate overall and by subregion, birth-country distribution of
resolved names vs. the matched subset), writes the supplement paragraph, and clears
the last analytical placeholders in the response letter.
