  1. KAROLINSKA INSTITUTET (GOVERNING BODY FOR PHYSIOLOGY/MEDICINE)
     Coverage: 1881–1970 only. Post-1970 Nobel Assembly roster is not
     publicly available. Wikidata has no P39/P463 records for Q3375124.
     Impact: All Physiology/Medicine governing body data missing after 1970.
     Records: 140 members, max endyear = 1970

  2. NOMINATION ARCHIVE 50-YEAR SECRECY RULE
     Physics/Chemistry/Literature: available through 1974 (or 1975).
     Peace: available through 1975.
     Physiology/Medicine: available through 1953 ONLY.
     Karolinska has not released 1954–1974 data (likely related to
     the 1977 Swedish transparency law and creation of the Nobel Assembly).

  3. NOBEL COMMITTEE FOR PHYSIOLOGY/MEDICINE (VETTING BODY)
     Source: Swedish Wikipedia page explicitly states
     'Listan är ofullständig' (the list is incomplete).
     Records: 72 members

  4. NOBEL COMMITTEE FOR LITERATURE (VETTING BODY)
     Source: svenskaakademien.se Nuxt.js SPA (rebuilt ~2024).
     Extraction parses __NUXT_DATA__ payload — fragile to redesigns.
     Records: 35 members with Nobel Committee service years

  5. NOMINATION PEOPLE QID MATCHING
     Total nomination archive people: 14474
     Matched to Wikidata QIDs: 3951 (27.3%)
     Missing birth year (unmatchable): 9904 (68.4%)
     Impact: Most nominator→nominee edges use temporary NOM: prefix IDs
     instead of Wikidata QIDs, limiting cross-layer entity resolution.

  6. RSAS ENDYEAR IMPUTATION
     RSAS members' endyear is imputed from Wikidata death_year (script 06).
     Still missing after imputation: 441 / 2312 (19.1%)
     These are likely living members whose actual departure date is unknown.

  7. STORTING TERM CONSOLIDATION
     Norwegian Parliament members may serve non-consecutive terms.
     Script 01 consolidates consecutive terms but gaps between terms
     are preserved. Verify that endyear reflects actual departure.
